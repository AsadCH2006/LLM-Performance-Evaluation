"Human activities, principally through emissions of greenhouse gases, have unequivocally caused global warming, with global surface temperature reaching 1.1°C above 1850–1900 in 2011–2020. Land-based regions have warmed faster than the oceans. Vulnerable communities who have historically contributed the least to global emissions are disproportionately affected by severe weather events, acute food and water insecurity, and loss of local livelihoods. Current climate adaptation strategies face severe roadblocks, primarily driven by a widening global climate finance deficit, critical regional data gaps, and a lack of integrated infrastructure planning."
